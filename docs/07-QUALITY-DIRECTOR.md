# Quality Director

## Score every generated clip
0–10 for:
- Camera Stability
- Environmental Life
- Property Fidelity
- Sharpness
- Luxury Feel
- Consistency

## Automatic decision
9.0+:
- accept

8.0–8.9:
- accept only if it fits the rest of the walkthrough

Below 8.0:
- regenerate

## Force-regenerate
Regardless of score:
- floating camera
- property changed
- visible architecture warping
- dead scene where obvious motion should exist
- opening blur
- major flicker

## Consistency
The most important rule:

Compare every accepted clip to the BEST clip in the walkthrough.

If the best clip feels professional and another clip feels like a moving still photo, the weaker clip should not survive just because it technically scored 8/10.

The final video should feel like ONE shoot.
