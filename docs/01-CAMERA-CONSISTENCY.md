# Camera Consistency Rules

## Core rule
Each generated clip gets exactly ONE camera movement.

Allowed:
- left to right
- right to left
- subtle zoom in
- subtle zoom out

Never combine multiple camera movements in a single clip.

## Forbidden movement
- vertical rise
- vertical drop
- tilt up
- tilt down
- orbit
- roll
- crane
- diagonal move
- floating
- bobbing
- aggressive dolly
- fast zoom
- perspective warping

## Movement amount
Movement should be subtle.

The goal is:
"real estate videographer with a stabilized camera"

Not:
"AI camera flying through the room"

## Suggested pattern
Exterior:
- left to right OR right to left

Living room:
- subtle zoom out

Kitchen:
- lateral slide

Bedroom:
- subtle zoom out

Bathroom:
- almost locked or extremely small lateral movement

View / pool / dock:
- lateral slide

## Consistency rule
Do not randomly choose a completely different motion style for every clip.

The walkthrough should alternate gently between:
- lateral movement
- zoom movement

No more than 2 movement styles should dominate the final video.
