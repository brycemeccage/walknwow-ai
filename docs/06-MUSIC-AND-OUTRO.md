# Music and Realtor Outro

## Music
Background music should feel premium and stay secondary to the property.

Suggested styles:
- Luxury -> cinematic ambient
- Waterfront -> spacious ambient
- Modern -> clean electronic
- Cabin -> warm acoustic
- Urban -> modern electronic
- Family -> warm lifestyle

Music:
- fade in ~1.5 seconds
- fade out ~2 seconds
- lower to about 30% during realtor outro

## Closing page
Target duration:
- ~6 seconds

Include:
- Realtor name
- Realtor title
- Brokerage
- Phone
- Email
- Website
- Brokerage logo (optional)
- Headshot (optional)
- QR code (optional)
- CTA: "Schedule Your Private Tour"

The outro should fade in smoothly from the final property shot.
