# Definition of Done

WalkNWow V1 is ready to sell when a realtor can:

1. Paste a listing URL.
2. AI analyzes all photos.
3. AI automatically selects ~16–20 strong scenes.
4. Every generated clip has one controlled camera movement.
5. Obvious environmental elements come alive naturally.
6. Weak clips automatically regenerate.
7. No clip looks dramatically worse than the best clip.
8. Clips transition with smooth 0.7-second dissolves.
9. Music is added automatically.
10. Realtor information appears on a polished closing card.
11. The final Luxury export is sharp 2K.
12. Realtor downloads the finished MP4 without manually editing anything.

If those 12 conditions work reliably, the core paid product exists.
